# Salt's Inventory Compatibility — 0.1.3

- Adds Simply Swords' Runic Forge on Minecraft 1.20.1 Fabric and Forge, with charcoal bars and native torn stone artwork. Preserves weapon/gem/tablet editing, preview tooltips, glowing channels and the original take-to-apply workflow.

- Adds Chipped's seven workbenches on Minecraft 1.20.1 Fabric and Forge, with a matching brown workshop, material picker, searchable variants, scrollbar and native preview modes. Craft and Craft All convert existing inventory stacks in place, with stale selections and workbench lifetime handled safely.
- Keeps the complete Unlink Gear grindstone artwork and Upgrade Gear armor-stand preview visible on Minecraft 1.20.1. Selected workstation tabs connect directly to their window frames at the left edge, and both grindstone views retain their full top outline.
- Adds compact Minecraft 1.20.1 desktop layouts for ExtendedDrawersAddon, BetterEnd, TieredZ, SmitherZ and AdditionZ on Fabric, The Graveyard containers and Runes on Fabric/Forge, and Deep Aether's Combiner on Forge.
- Preserves native recipes, processing, previews, upgrade controls and item rules in compact windows. Workstation tabs sit outside the frame; recipe-book toggles stay inside. Gravestone text editing keeps its normal sign-style screen.

- Prevents newer Alloyed worlds from failing to load when its optional Create-based knife recipe is unavailable. The recipe remains available when Alloyed, Farmer's Delight and Create are installed.
- Preserves the standalone Alloyed forge's processing and Alloy Forgery's newer slot locks and live indicators in their desktop windows.
- Extends these native templates to every later supported target with a matching upstream release, including the standalone Alloyed 3.x forge. Parchment identifying tables, in-frame recipe buttons and compact native controls follow the installed mod release.
- Adds desktop support for Iron Chests: Restocked and Animal Feeding Trough.
- Adds compact Minecraft 1.20.1 desktop layouts for Bartering Station, Farmer's Delight / Refabricated, Better Archeology, Illager Invasion, Galosphere and Sawmill on Fabric and Forge, plus Alloy Forgery and Fabric Seasons: Extras on Fabric and Miner's Delight on Forge.
- Preserves native processing indicators, recipe selection, search, fuel controls and item rules in these layouts. Cooking recipe buttons sit inside widened pot windows; Alloy Forgery's lava reservoir remains outside the main frame.
- Adds compact native layouts for Oxidized, Brick & Mortar, Expanded Delight, Crabber's Delight, Nether Chested and Inmis wherever matching upstream releases are available.
- Fits windows around their actual controls and slot grids, removing excess empty space. Larger layouts use scrolling when needed.
- Gives Better Archeology's identifying table a matching parchment frame and controls, while keeping its native artwork, slots and processing.
- Keeps Brick & Mortar's animated recipe preview outside the kiln's top border, with the main body staying in place as it opens.
- Preserves native artwork, processing, recipe controls, tooltips and item restrictions. Nether Chested uses standard inventory cell backgrounds while retaining large-stack counts and transfers.
- Supports every default Inmis backpack shape, including sorting in the three-slot Baby Backpack.
- Preserves feeding trough interactions and closes its desktop window when the source is destroyed, replaced or out of range.
- Supports sorting, transfers and resizing for all fifteen Iron Chests chest/barrel variants, including restricted Dirt Chest storage and larger Netherite grids.

Requires **Core 0.1.6** for the same Minecraft version and loader. Install the supported third-party mods and their dependencies separately. Fabric requires Fabric API. Upstream availability differs by Minecraft version and loader; see the compatibility page for the supported combinations.
