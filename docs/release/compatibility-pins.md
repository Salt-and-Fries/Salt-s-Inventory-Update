# Reviewed compatibility pins

Exact candidate verification and independent review status are recorded in [FINAL_VERIFICATION.md](FINAL_VERIFICATION.md). This table records registration and upstream pins; gameplay acceptance remains in the linked qualification evidence.

A dash means **no reviewed support pin in this inventory**, rather than a claim that the publisher has never released that target. Use the exact releases recorded in the [machine-readable table](compatibility-pins.csv); additional fixture variants are recorded in accepted runtime manifests.

Registered integrations adapt inventory/container interfaces. Other books, selectors and settings remain native where specified by the integration. Browser providers and native settings are labelled separately. Unregistered conventional containers may use Core's automatic fallback; that does not constitute dedicated compatibility or qualification.

Later integrations are included from exact local publisher fixtures and their integration guides. Multiple versions in one cell are distinct reviewed binary pins, not a claim that every intervening patch was tested. A link opens the exact publisher release when cached release metadata is available; otherwise it opens that publisher's release index. Exact hashes remain in the CSV. Fixture presence and registration do not establish final gameplay acceptance.

Required base mods, libraries, version constraints and declared side requirements are recorded in the [pinned dependency guide](UPSTREAM_DEPENDENCIES.md) and its complete CSV. These declarations do not grant gameplay acceptance.
